Markers on glass make it visible to birds: exterior markers cut a pane's strikes by about 90%, and cheaper interior ones by about 60%. Set a budget and the planner chooses, pane by pane, the treatments that prevent the most strikes. It counts a setup visit for each facade it touches and a lift for exterior work above the ground floor.
